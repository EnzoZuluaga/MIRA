# MIRA

## Monitoring Intelligence for Risk Anticipation

MIRA is a long-term research and engineering project focused on the development of an intelligent environmental monitoring and risk anticipation system.

The project aims to combine environmental sensors, satellite data, software engineering, artificial intelligence and scientific documentation to study and anticipate environmental risks such as floods, droughts and climate-related events.

MIRA is not intended to be a short-term prototype or a simple application.  
Its primary goal is to build a real, documented and verifiable technical trajectory over multiple years of continuous learning, experimentation and development.

---

# Vision

To develop a scalable environmental intelligence platform capable of integrating real-world data, satellite observation and predictive analysis in order to improve environmental risk awareness and anticipation.

---

# Core Objectives

- Learn software engineering and scientific programming
- Build a professional research workflow
- Develop strong technical documentation practices
- Integrate environmental and satellite datasets
- Design low-cost environmental sensing systems
- Explore AI-assisted environmental analysis
- Publish a transparent and verifiable development process

---

# Methodology

MIRA follows a continuous iterative workflow:

```text
Learn
→ Apply
→ Analyze
→ Improve
→ Document
→ Publish
```

The project prioritizes real understanding, technical consistency and long-term progression over rapid superficial development.
